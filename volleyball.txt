Volleyball is a team sport in which two teams of six players each are separated by a high net. Teams score points by hitting the ball over the net so that it lands on the floor of the other team's side of the court. Players may touch the ball with any part of the body, and each team is allowed up to three touches before the ball must cross back over the net. Volleyball was invented in 1895 by William Morgan as an indoor sport, and it has been part of the Summer Olympic Games since 1964. A match is usually played as the best of five sets. Players rotate positions on the court, and the server must hit the ball from behind the end line. Common skills include serving, passing, setting, and spiking the ball.
