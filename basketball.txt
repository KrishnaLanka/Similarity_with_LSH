Basketball is a team sport in which two teams of five players each try to score points by shooting a ball through a hoop. The hoop is mounted on a backboard at each end of a rectangular court. Players move the ball by dribbling and passing, and score two points for most baskets or three points for a shot taken from behind the three point line. Basketball was invented in 1891 by James Naismith, a physical education teacher who wanted an indoor game to keep his students active during the winter. The sport is now popular worldwide and has been part of the Summer Olympic Games since 1936. Common skills include dribbling, shooting, passing, defense, running, and jumping. A game is divided into timed periods, and the team with the most points at the end wins.
